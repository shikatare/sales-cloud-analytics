Machine Learning

Six models

Sales data carries a skew of 12.98, so a log-transform was applied before training. Each model was tuned with Optuna Bayesian search across 30 trials.

01
Random Forest
Ensemble of decision trees with bootstrap aggregation. Strong baseline with good feature importance interpretability.
sklearn
02
Gradient Boosting
Sequential boosting that corrects previous errors. Handles non-linear patterns in sales data effectively.
sklearn
03
XGBoost
Extreme gradient boosting with L1 and L2 regularization. Fast and consistently accurate on tabular data.
xgboost
04
LightGBM
Leaf-wise tree growth for faster training. Particularly effective on high-cardinality categorical features.
lightgbm
05
Stacking Ensemble
Meta-learner combining predictions from all base models. Captures diverse signal that single models miss.
ensemble
06
Customer Segmentation
KMeans on RFM features produced four distinct customer groups for targeted business strategy.
kmeans
